life-butler

life-butler 是作者自己使用的生活规划工具：每晚根据目标和日历排出第二天的计划，白天随突发情况重排。它运行在作者自己的电脑上，不对外提供服务，也不接受其他用户注册。

life-butler is a personal, self-hosted daily-planning tool used only by its author. Every evening it drafts the next day's plan from the author's goals and calendar, and it re-plans during the day when things change. It runs on the author's own computer and is not offered as a service to anyone else.
